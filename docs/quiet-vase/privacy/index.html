Quiet Vase: Bouquet Arranging

Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026
Last updated: 22 November 2026

Developer and publisher:
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

Android package: com.NikfortGames.quiet_vase
Privacy contact: nikfortgames@gmail.com

Your bouquets are stored on your device. Quiet Vase has no app account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated cloud sync.

1. Information stored on your device

Quiet Vase stores your Studio draft and saved bouquets in Android app-private storage. This includes names you enter, dates, vases, palettes, stem placements, Harmony results and preview images. The app stores preferences such as theme, sound and onboarding progress. It uses these details to restore your Studio, display your album and create files you request. Nikfort Games does not receive your drafts, bouquets or preferences and does not sell personal information.

2. Optional Google Play purchase

Google Play processes the one-time Studio Collection purchase through your Google Play account. The app receives product and purchase information, including a purchase token, to check and restore access and acknowledge the purchase with Google. The app caches the unlock status on your device and excludes it from backup files. Quiet Vase does not receive your payment card details or send bouquet data to Google Play Billing. Google handles purchase and payment records under the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Backups, exports and sharing

You can save or restore a JSON backup through Android’s document picker. It includes your saved album and portable settings. Restoring replaces the current album and settings. The Studio draft remains separate. Shared PNGs contain your bouquet artwork. PDF catalogues include the artwork, names, dates and Harmony results of the bouquets you select.

You choose where files are saved and which app receives a shared image. A destination can be a cloud provider and handles its copies under its own terms. Quiet Vase does not password-protect these files. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the app configuration. Device-to-device transfer behavior can vary by device.

4. Retention and deletion

Saved bouquets stay in the album until you delete them or replace the album from a backup. Clear the vase to remove the stems from your Studio draft. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Delete exported files, backups and shared copies at their destinations. Clearing app storage does not delete Google Play purchase history or support correspondence.

5. Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information or files you send. We use them to respond and keep correspondence as needed for support or legal obligations. For privacy questions or to request access to or deletion of correspondence we hold, email nikfortgames@gmail.com. We cannot access or remotely erase your in-app files.

6. This website and policy updates

Quiet Vase opens this policy in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the policy and processes information needed to serve the page under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Nikfort Games adds no advertising or analytics scripts to this page. If these practices change, we will update this page and the last-updated date.
