Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Developer and publisher:
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

Android package: com.NikfortGames.quiet_coils
Privacy contact: nikfortgames@gmail.com

Your artwork stays on your device until you choose to export or share it. Quiet Coils has no app accounts, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated cloud sync.

1. Information stored on your device

Quiet Coils uses Android app-private storage for your open Studio draft, saved artwork, card names and dates, composition readings, and cached thumbnails. It stores preferences such as theme, sound, and onboarding progress to reopen your Studio the way you left it. Nikfort Games does not receive this information or sell personal information. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the app configuration. Device-to-device transfer behavior can vary by device.

2. Optional Google Play purchase

Google Play processes the one-time Paper Collection purchase. The app uses product and purchase information returned by Google Play, including a purchase token, to confirm, acknowledge, and restore access. The app stores the unlock status on the device so you can use previously confirmed access offline. Backup files exclude this status. Quiet Coils does not receive your payment card details or send your artwork to Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and purchase records under the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Backups, exports, and sharing

A JSON backup contains saved artworks and portable app settings. Restoring it replaces the Album and those settings and keeps the open draft separate. PNG cards and PDF catalogue pages include artwork, card names, and dates. The app creates these files on your device. When you use the Android document picker or share sheet, you choose the destination. It may be another app, a recipient, or a cloud provider that handles its copy under its own terms. Quiet Coils does not password-protect exported files.

4. Retention and deletion

Saved cards stay in the Album until you delete them or replace the Album from a backup. You can clear the open Studio board in the app. To remove all local app data, including cached images and preferences, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Delete backups, exports, and shared copies separately at their destinations. Google Play maintains its own purchase records.

5. Support and privacy questions

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and keep correspondence as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of correspondence we hold, email nikfortgames@gmail.com. We cannot access or remotely delete your local app files.

6. This website and policy updates

The app opens this policy in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts it and processes information needed to serve the page under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Nikfort Games adds no advertising or analytics scripts to this page. We update this page and its last-updated date when these practices change.
