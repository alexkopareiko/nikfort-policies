Your canning records are not sent to us. Canning has no account or sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your canning records or send them to developer-operated servers.

1. Information handled by the app

Canning has no account or sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your canning records or send them to developer-operated servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores information locally so its features can work, including:

  • Your elevation, canner setup, preferences, recent foods, and favourites.
  • Batch records, notes, processing progress, timer deadlines, and seal checks.
  • Pantry quantities, storage locations, usage history, and quality targets.
  • Freezer records and any costs or store prices you enter.
  • Reminder settings and local purchase-unlock status.

We cannot access these records through the app.

3. Android backups and exported files

Depending on your device and Android backup settings, app data may be included in your personal device backup. Your backup provider controls that service.

When you save a backup, CSV report, or PDF, the app writes the file to the destination you choose through Android's file picker. That destination may be local storage or a cloud-storage provider.

When restoring a backup, the app reads the file you select. We do not receive your backup or exported files. Their storage and any sharing you initiate are subject to your chosen provider's terms and privacy policy.

4. Notifications and timers

Processing timers and reminders are scheduled locally on your device using the timer deadlines and reminder settings you enter. You control notification permission and lock-screen visibility in Android settings.

5. Access and deletion

You can remove individual records in the app or remove its local data through Android's app-storage settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app data. Exported files and backup copies may remain until you delete them separately.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nikfortgames@gmail.com.
