Aquarium Water Log

Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026
Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Aquarium Water Log, an offline aquarium journal with an optional Pro unlock.

Developer and Publisher:
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

App: Aquarium Water Log
Android package: com.NikfortGames.aquarium_water_log
Privacy contact: nikfortgames@gmail.com

Your aquarium records stay on your device. The app has no account and does not send your tank or test data to us. Optional purchases, device backups and files you choose to export involve the services described below.

  1. Records on your device
  2. Android backups and backup files
  3. Purchases
  4. Internet access
  5. Pro features and exports
  6. Sharing of information
  7. Children's privacy
  8. Changes to this policy
  9. Contact

1. Records on your device

The app stores your tank and test data, settings, and whether the full version is unlocked in the app's files and preferences on your phone. This data does not leave your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

2. Android backups and backup files

If you use Android's built-in backup, your device may include app data in your personal Google backup. Google and your device settings control that backup, not us.

When you tap Save backup file, the app writes your tank and test data as plain JSON to a location you choose using Android's file picker, such as your phone storage or Google Drive. To restore, the app reads only the file you select. We never receive these files.

3. Purchases

The optional Pro version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card details, name, or address. The app receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid so it can unlock Pro and restore it after reinstallation.

See Google's Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles payment data.

4. Internet access

All aquarium features work fully offline. The app communicates with Google Play only when you open the purchase, restore, or price-check flow, and only to confirm that purchase. Unlocking Pro does not send us any data: your purchase state stays on your device.

5. Pro features and exports

Pro features (long-range trend analytics, custom parameters, CSV history export) run entirely on your device. The CSV files you export are written by Android's file picker to a location you choose; we never receive them.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information because we do not collect any.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults maintaining aquariums and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nikfortgames@gmail.com.
