Journal records and settings

The app keeps your projects, wood and pattern names, dates, notes, burner rigs, burn samples, correction history, sessions and cost entries in private app storage on your device. Session records can keep a snapshot of the setting used. The app also stores unfinished drafts, display and journal preferences, and a local Pro unlock flag. You can use the journal without creating an account. We operate no journal sync or collection server and include no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

Files you choose

Android’s document picker gives the app access to the individual files and destinations you select. The transfer tool reads your selected PNG or JPEG to generate a PDF. It can retain permission and a reference to reopen the selected file, along with print dimensions and paper settings. It leaves the original in its location. The app does not request broad access to photos or files, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

JSON backups can contain saved records, Trash, correction history, session snapshots and journal preferences. They exclude the separate draft store, pattern image files, print preferences and Pro entitlement. Text, CSV and PDF exports contain information from the records or artwork you choose to export. These files have no app-applied password protection. If you choose a cloud folder, send a file or print through an external service, that destination or service handles the file under its own terms.

Google Play purchases

Google Play Billing handles optional Pro purchases and restoration. Google processes the transaction and payment details under its Privacy Policy. The app uses purchase status to unlock Pro. We do not receive your card details in the app or send your journal to Google Play Billing.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device while you keep them. Moving an item to Trash retains it for restoration, and correcting a sample retains its previous values in the history. You can discard unfinished drafts or replace saved journal records by confirming a restore. Clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its private data from that installation. The app disables Android automatic backup in its manifest. Android and device manufacturers control device-to-device migration behavior. Delete exported copies and original source files wherever you stored them. Removing local data does not delete Google Play transaction records.

Website and support

The privacy link opens in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts this site and handles web requests under the GitHub privacy statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and retain correspondence as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. Please avoid sending private journal entries or payment details. Contact nikfortgames@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
