Small Engine Service Log

Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026
Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Small Engine Service Log handles information when you record equipment use and maintenance on Android.

Developer and publisher:
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

App: Small Engine Service Log
Android package: com.NikfortGames.small_engine_service_log
Privacy contact: nikfortgames@gmail.com

Your equipment log is stored on your device. The app has no account or developer-operated cloud sync. Google Play purchases, files you choose to save or share, and support email are described below.

1. Equipment records and drafts

The app stores machine names, equipment types, manufacturer, model and serial details, dated engine-hour readings and corrections, service work, parts and fluids, service providers, costs, notes, targets, reminder settings and unfinished drafts. It uses these records to maintain your log, calculate totals and due dates, and generate reports when you request them.

Records and drafts are stored in Android app-private storage. The app does not send your log to Nikfort Games, and it has no advertising, analytics, or developer-operated crash-reporting service. We do not sell your information. Free-text fields can contain personal or business information that you choose to enter.

2. Local reminders

If you set a Pro reminder, the app uses the date or engine-hour threshold you enter to display a local notification. On Android 13 and later, the app asks for notification permission when you choose to enable notifications. A notification can show the machine name on your lock screen, depending on your Android settings. You can remove a reminder inside the app or disable notifications in Android settings.

3. Optional Google Play purchase

Google Play processes the optional one-time Pro purchase using your Google Play account. Through Google Play Billing, the app receives product and purchase information, including a purchase token, to check and restore access and acknowledge the purchase with Google. The app keeps the resulting unlock status on the device and excludes it from backup files. The app does not receive your payment card details or send equipment records to Google Play Billing. Google handles its purchase and payment records under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Backups, exports, and sharing

You can save a JSON backup using Android’s document picker. It includes equipment records, corrections, voided entries, targets, reminders and drafts. Restoring a backup replaces the current log with the selected file. Pro PDF and CSV exports can contain readings, dates, machine names, service work, parts, providers, costs and notes from the equipment and dates you select.

You choose where files are saved and which app or recipient receives a shared report. A destination may be a cloud storage provider. Its handling of the file follows its own privacy terms. The app does not password-protect backups or exported reports. Android automatic app backup is disabled in the app’s configuration. Device-to-device transfer behavior can depend on Android and the device manufacturer.

5. Retention and deletion

The app keeps local records while you use the log. Deleting a machine removes its readings, services, settings, reminders and drafts from the active log. Discarding a draft removes that draft. A corrected reading or voided service entry is retained in the log for its history while the machine remains. You can clear all local app data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

To remove exported reports, backups or copies sent to another service, delete those copies at their destinations. Deleting app data does not remove Google Play transaction records or support emails. Android app-private storage limits ordinary access by other apps. Use your device’s screen lock and review what you include before sharing a file.

6. Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, the information you include and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and keep a record of the correspondence as needed for support or legal obligations. You can ask about the information we hold or request deletion by emailing nikfortgames@gmail.com. We cannot access or remotely delete your local log. The app is intended for equipment owners and maintainers and is not directed to children.

7. Website and policy changes

The app opens this policy in your browser. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes information needed to serve the page under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics or advertising scripts to this page.

If these practices change, we will update this page and its last-updated date.
