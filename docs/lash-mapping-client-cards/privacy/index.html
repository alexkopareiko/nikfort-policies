1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter locally on your device, including:

  • Client names, optional contact details, and notes.
  • Visit dates, service types, lash maps, layers, and measurements.
  • Copies of reference photos you choose to attach to visits.
  • Saved templates and unfinished visit drafts.
  • Settings and whether Pro features are unlocked.

We do not operate a server that receives your client records. The app does not require an account or sign-in and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Please enter only information you are authorized to keep. You are responsible for informing your clients and obtaining consent where required.

2. Exports and sharing

When you create a PDF or PNG working card, the app generates the file on your device. Depending on your export choices, the file may include client names, maps, and notes.

Files are saved to the location you choose or shared through an app you select using Android's sharing tools. A cloud storage provider or messaging service you choose may send the file to its servers. That service's privacy policy then applies.

We do not receive copies of these exports. You are responsible for choosing appropriate recipients and protecting exported files.

3. Reference photos

When you select a reference photo using Android's photo picker, the app saves a smaller private copy with the visit. The copy is resized, its orientation is normalized, and embedded metadata such as location is removed. Your gallery original is unchanged.

The app does not analyze faces or infer treatment recommendations from photos. Private copies are removed when they are no longer needed by a saved visit or unfinished draft. Gallery originals are never deleted by the app.

4. Full backup files

When you choose Save full backup, the app creates a ZIP file containing your client records, visits, saved templates, reference-photo copies, studio name, and unfinished visit draft. You choose where to save it using Android's file picker.

Backup files are unencrypted and may contain personal information, so keep them in private storage you trust. Saving a backup to a cloud provider sends the file to that provider, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive the file.

Restoring a backup replaces the app's current client book after you confirm. Backups do not contain payment credentials and do not grant Pro ownership. Deleting records in the app does not delete copies in previously saved backup files; manage those files separately.

5. Google Play purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. The app exchanges purchase-related information with Google Play to display prices, process and acknowledge purchases, verify ownership, and restore access.

The app receives purchase status and transaction identifiers needed for these functions. We do not receive your payment card details. Google handles payment and related information under its own privacy policy.

6. Internet access

Client recordkeeping and map editing work offline. Google Play purchase, pricing, and restoration functions require communication with Google Play. Services you choose for sharing or saving exports may also use the internet.

7. Retention and deletion

You can delete visits, clients, and their associated records in the app, or delete the entire client book from Settings. Private photo copies are removed when no saved visit or unfinished draft needs them. Archiving a client keeps their records; it does not delete them.

Local records remain on your device until removed. You can delete the app's local records and settings by clearing its storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. The current build disables Android automatic backup and device transfer for app data.

Deleting the app does not delete files you exported, copies held by recipients, backup files you saved, or Google Play transaction records. Manage those separately through the relevant location or service.

8. Security

The app uses Android's private app storage for its local records. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software updated. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use this information to respond and provide support. Please avoid sending identifiable client records unless necessary.

10. Children

The app is intended for professional lash recordkeeping and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The revised policy will show an updated effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent us, email nikfortgames@gmail.com.
