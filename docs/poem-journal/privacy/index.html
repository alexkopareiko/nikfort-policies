Poem Journal: Quiet Writing

Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026
Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy applies to Poem Journal: Quiet Writing, an Android app for writing and keeping a private poetry journal.

Developer and Publisher:
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

Android package: com.NikfortGames.poem_journal
Privacy contact: nikfortgames@gmail.com

Your writing stays on your device unless you export it. Poem Journal has no app account, cloud sync, advertising or analytics.

Your journal on this device

The app saves your poem titles and text, drafts, tags, collection names, pen names, and preferences in Android’s app-private storage. If you use the Pro submission ledger, it also keeps the markets, dates, fees, currencies, statuses, notes and change history you enter. A submission entry is a private record. The app does not contact the market or send it your poem.

We do not receive your journal or have remote access to it. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. Android’s app storage separates the journal from ordinary access by other apps. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the app configuration.

Backups and exports you choose

When you export a poem, PDF, submission CSV or JSON backup, Android’s file picker asks where to save it. When you restore a backup, the app reads the file you select and previews its contents before you confirm replacement. A JSON backup can contain your complete journal, including drafts, Trash and submission history. Exported files are not encrypted or password-protected by Poem Journal.

If you choose a cloud folder or send an exported file to another person or service, that destination controls the copy under its own terms. Poem Journal does not upload journal content to the developer.

Optional Google Play purchase

Google Play processes the optional one-time Pro purchase, including payment and Google account information, under the Google Privacy Policy. The app receives purchase status and a purchase token through Google Play Billing to confirm, acknowledge or restore the entitlement. The app keeps the unlock status on your device. It does not receive your card number, send your purchase token to a developer server or include the unlock in journal backups. Google Play manages its own transaction records.

Retention and deletion

Saved writing and drafts remain in app storage until you remove or replace them. Moving a saved poem to Trash keeps the poem and its unfinished edits available to restore. You can discard an unfinished draft in the editor. Removing a ledger entry hides it from the ledger and preserves a deletion event and prior details in the journal. When replacing the journal from a backup, the app keeps one local copy of the previous journal for recovery.

To remove the entire local journal, Trash, history and recovery copy, clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Delete any backups or exports separately wherever you stored them. Clearing local storage does not delete Google Play’s purchase records. There is no Poem Journal account to close.

Support and privacy requests

Email nikfortgames@gmail.com with questions or requests about information you have sent us. When you email, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond and retain them as needed to handle the request, keep necessary support records or meet legal obligations. Please leave private poems and payment details out of your message unless they are needed to explain the issue.

Visiting this page and policy updates

The in-app Privacy policy link opens your browser. GitHub Pages hosts this website and handles technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page includes no analytics scripts or contact forms. If our practices change, the current policy and revision date will appear at this same address.
