1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • Your settings (inches or millimetres, fraction step)
  • The roof you last entered (span, pitch, overhangs, lumber sizes, spacing)
  • Jobs you save, with their names
  • How many times the app was opened, so it can show the Pro offer once, and whether you closed that offer
  • Whether Pro is unlocked

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

If you use Android’s built-in backup, your device may include this app data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

3. Voice readout

The app uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone to read marks aloud. The app sends that engine only the text to be spoken. The app does not use the microphone and does not record anything.

4. PDF cut sheets and sharing

When you tap Share PDF cut sheet, the app creates the PDF on your device and opens Android’s share menu. The file goes only to the app or person you choose. We never receive it.

5. Backup files

When you tap Save backup file, the app writes your saved jobs and settings to a file in the place you choose with Android’s file picker (for example your phone’s storage or Google Drive). When you restore, the app reads only the file you pick. We never receive these files.

6. Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card details, name or address. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro and restore it after reinstalling.

Google’s handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Internet access

The app works fully offline. The only network communication is between the app and Google Play for checking the price and purchases.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nikfortgames@gmail.com.
