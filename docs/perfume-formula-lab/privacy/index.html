1. Local records

The app uses information you enter to calculate weigh-outs and batch costs and maintain your formula book. Records can include formula names, percentages, notes, materials, stock concentrations, carriers, prices, optional supplier densities, unfinished drafts, and display settings. Pro tools can also store previous formula versions and dated trial and batch records, including cost snapshots, notes, corrections and void reasons.

The app keeps these records in Android app-private storage. It does not transmit the formula book to Nikfort Games. Automatic Android backup is disabled in the app configuration. The app provides manual ZIP backup for transferring your book.

2. Files and sharing

You can choose a material CSV to import, save or restore a ZIP backup, share a formula as plain text, and export PDF or CSV with Pro. Android lets you select the file provider, destination or receiving app. A cloud storage or sharing provider you choose may handle your files or text under its own privacy practices.

ZIP backups can include drafts, settings and history. The app does not password-protect or encrypt these ZIP files. Store them in a location you trust and share them only with intended recipients.

3. Google Play purchases

When Pro is available, it is an optional one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app uses Google Play Billing purchase and ownership information to unlock Pro, acknowledge purchases and restore access. The app stores the local Pro access status separately from the formula book and excludes it from ZIP backups. The app does not ask for or receive payment-card details. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles transactions and related information.

4. Retention and deletion

Deleting a current formula, material or draft removes that record from the active book. Saved formula rows keep their material snapshots. Deleting a formula does not remove retained version or trial records. Trial corrections retain the original record, and voiding marks the record in the history.

Restoring a ZIP replaces the active book after validation and confirmation. The app retains one previous book privately for recovery. It may contain records that were replaced or later deleted.

To remove all private app copies from your device, clear the app's storage from Android settings or uninstall the app. Copies saved, exported or shared outside the app must be deleted separately from the service or location where they were stored. Deleting local app data does not delete Google Play transaction records.

5. Website and support

The app opens this policy in your browser. GitHub Pages processes technical request information to serve the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page uses no advertising or analytics scripts.

For support or privacy requests, contact nikfortgames@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your email address and the information you include to respond and keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request. You can contact us to ask about deletion of that correspondence. Please include only the information needed for us to help.

6. Policy changes

We update this page if the app's data practices change. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
