Stained Glass Calculator

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated 20 November 2026

Developer and publisher
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

Android package
com.NikfortGames.stained_glass

Information stored on your device

The app keeps projects, unfinished drafts, estimates, settings, glass inventory, customer names and quote details, and job costs and revenue in its private Android storage. You do not need an app account. The developer does not operate a server that receives these records. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles the optional one-time Pro purchase and its restoration. Google processes your Play account and payment information. The app receives purchase status and a purchase token through Google Play Billing to check ownership, acknowledge the purchase with Google Play, and unlock or restore Pro. The app stores the unlocked status locally. The developer may receive order information through Play Console for order management. The app does not receive your payment card details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Sharing and backups

You can share estimates, export quotes or job data, and save or restore a ZIP backup. A ZIP can contain drafts, customer names, prices and other studio records. Android lets you choose where files go and which app receives shared content. The provider you choose may sync those files. Android’s system backup or device transfer may also copy eligible app data according to your device settings.

How information is protected

Android restricts routine access to the app’s private storage. The app does not add password protection or encryption to exported ZIP, CSV or PDF files. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read it. Keep exports containing customer or pricing information in a location you trust.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until you delete or replace them, or clear the app’s data. To remove all local records and settings, open Android Settings, select Stained Glass Calculator under Apps, and clear its storage. The app has no developer-hosted account or studio records to delete. A separate backup or device transfer can restore previously copied data. Separately delete any exported or backed-up files from their destinations if you no longer want them. Clearing the app does not cancel a purchase or delete Google Play’s purchase history.

External sites and support

The privacy link opens your browser. GitHub Pages hosts this site and processes technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include to respond to your request. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle, document or follow up on the request, or as required by law. You can use the same contact to request deletion of your correspondence.

For privacy questions, email nikfortgames@gmail.com. We post policy updates at this address.
