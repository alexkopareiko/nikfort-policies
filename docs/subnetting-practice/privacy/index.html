1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves only the data needed for its features:

  • Your mistakes, for Mistake review
  • Your best Exam mode result
  • Whether the full version is unlocked
  • Whether you have seen the first-question hint

This data stays on your device and is not visible to us. You can delete it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Android backup

If you use Android’s built-in backup, your device may include this app data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

4. Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card details, name, or address. The app receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock the full version and restore it after reinstalling.

Google’s handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Internet access

The app works fully offline. Its only network communication is with Google Play to check prices and purchases.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information because we do not collect any.

7. Children’s privacy

This educational app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nikfortgames@gmail.com.
