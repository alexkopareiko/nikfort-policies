1. Records and settings on your device

The app stores project names, weaving dimensions, densities, loom allowances, yarn quantities, weights and prices, and unfinished drafts to calculate estimates and reopen your work. Optional Pro tools store yarn names, dye-lot notes, stock quantities and yarn assignments, plus the actual yarn usage and costs you record. These records support stash checks, shopping lists and comparisons with estimates.

The app keeps these records in Android app-private storage. It stores settings such as measurement units, appearance and project defaults on the device, and reads the phone locale for number and currency formatting. The calculator can run offline. The app includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own and does not request access to contacts, location, photos, the camera or microphone.

2. Optional Google Play purchases

Weaving Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles checkout, payment details and the transaction under its privacy policy. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app.

Google Play Billing looks up the product and price and returns purchase and ownership information. The app uses a purchase token through Google Play Billing when acknowledging a purchase and stores the Pro entitlement on the device to show the appropriate features. It queries Google Play to check or restore ownership. Google may process transaction, account, device and diagnostic information in providing its services.

3. Backups, exports and sharing

You can create a ZIP backup containing saved projects, drafts, stash records, project yarn assignments and recorded actuals. The app reads a ZIP only when you select it to restore, and a valid restore replaces the current ledger. The ZIP does not carry the Google Play purchase entitlement. The app disables Android automatic backup in its manifest. Device manufacturers and Android transfer features may behave differently.

You can share a project calculation as text through Android's share sheet. Pro can export shopping lists and cost reports as CSV or PDF. The text or files may include project or yarn names, calculations, costs, quantities or dye-lot notes, depending on what you export. Choose the receiving app, recipient or save location. An app or cloud provider you choose handles the information under its own policies.

The app does not add encryption or password protection to exported files. Use a destination you trust and protect any copies containing private information.

4. Retention, deletion and security

Projects and drafts stay on the device until you delete them, replace the ledger with a valid backup, or remove the app's data. In Projects, use Delete on a project or draft and confirm. This removes the project and any associated draft from the current ledger. In Yarn stash, deleting a yarn also clears its project assignments.

Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes the local app data. These actions do not delete copies you saved elsewhere, shared messages, or purchase history kept by Google Play. Delete those copies in the destination where you saved or sent them. The developer cannot access or delete your on-device weaving records remotely.

Android's app-private storage restricts other apps' access to local records. Your device lock and the security of any chosen destination help protect your data.

5. Platform services and this website

Android, Google Play and your device provider may collect platform or diagnostic information separately under their own settings and terms. This policy site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub handles technical request information to serve the website under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The policy page adds no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or contact forms.

6. Support and privacy requests

Email nikfortgames@gmail.com and mention Weaving Calculator & Yarn. We receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer questions, investigate issues or handle privacy requests. The mailbox is hosted by Gmail.

Support messages remain in the mailbox until deleted. You can request correction or deletion of information you sent by identifying the conversation. We may retain information needed to resolve an ongoing issue or meet applicable legal obligations. A parent or guardian can contact us about information sent to support.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data-handling practices change. The current policy and its latest revision date will remain available at this address.
