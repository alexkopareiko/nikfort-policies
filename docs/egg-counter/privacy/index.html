1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following data only on your own device:

  • your flocks and hens (names, kind of bird, hen counts and changes such as birds added, sold or lost)
  • your egg log (eggs per day and egg type, and the weather and notes you add)
  • your expenses, egg sales and eggs used
  • your hatches (dates, egg counts, temperature and humidity readings and notes)
  • your settings (currency, temperature unit, reminder time)
  • whether Egg Counter Pro is unlocked

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

If you use Android’s built-in backup, your device may include this app data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

3. Home-screen widget

If you add the app’s widget, it shows today’s egg count on your home screen and adds an egg when you tap +1. It uses the same data stored on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

4. Backup files

When you tap “Save backup file,” the app writes your data to a file in the place you choose with Android’s file picker (for example your phone’s storage or Google Drive). When you restore, the app reads only the file you pick. We never receive these files.

5. Reports and exports

CSV files and PDF reports are created on your device and saved or shared where you choose. They go only to the places and people you choose. We never receive them.

6. Notifications

If you switch reminders on (the daily reminder to log your eggs, or hatch reminders such as candling and lockdown days), the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone. Reminders are created and shown on your device only; nothing is sent to a server. You can switch them off in the app or in your phone’s settings at any time.

7. Purchases

Egg Counter Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card details, name or address. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro and restore it after reinstalling.

Google’s handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Internet access

The app works fully offline. The only network communication is between the app and Google Play for checking prices and purchases.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

10. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nikfortgames@gmail.com.
