Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Updated 20 November 2026

Developer and publisher
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

Android package
com.NikfortGames.balusterspacing

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your settings, most recent measurements, saved Pro projects and runs, and Pro unlock status in private Android storage. You do not need an app account. Nikfort Games does not operate a server that receives these records. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Voice readout and sharing

When you use Pro voice readout, the app sends the text of layout marks to Android’s installed text-to-speech engine. The engine handles speech according to its own settings and privacy policy. The app does not use the microphone or record audio.

When you export a layout as an image or PDF, the app creates the file on your device and opens Android’s share menu. You choose the recipient or destination. The receiving service may store or sync the file.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles the optional one-time Pro purchase and restoration. The app receives purchase status and a purchase token to check ownership, acknowledge your purchase, and unlock or restore Pro. The app does not receive payment card details. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Backups, retention and deletion

Android backup or device transfer may copy eligible app data according to your device settings. You can remove saved projects in the app and clear all local app data in Android Settings. Delete exported files and backups separately from their destinations. Clearing app storage does not delete your Google Play purchase history.

External sites and support

The privacy link opens your browser. GitHub Pages hosts this site and handles technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond.

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. For privacy questions, email nikfortgames@gmail.com. We post policy updates on this page.
