Your puzzle progress stays on your device. You can play without an app account. The game has no ads, analytics, or developer-operated cloud sync.

Puzzle progress and settings

The game saves yarn placements, in-progress rounds, completed patterns, stars, badges, and Quilt Wall progress in Android app-private storage. It also saves settings such as sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, large text, and tutorial progress. The game uses these records to resume your puzzles and apply your preferences. Pattern of the Day uses the date calculated on your device. Nikfort Games does not receive these records.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. There is no in-app artwork export or sharing feature.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles optional one-time purchases through your Google Play account. The game receives product identifiers, purchase status, and a purchase token from Google Play to check, acknowledge, and restore purchases. It saves which products you own in app-private storage so it can remember access between sessions. It does not send your puzzle progress or preferences to Google Play Billing, and it does not receive your payment card details. Google handles payment and purchase records under the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Android restricts access to the app’s private storage. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in the app configuration. Device-to-device transfer behavior may vary by device. Clear the game’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove locally saved progress, settings, and cached ownership from the device. This does not erase Google Play purchase history. You can restore eligible purchases through Google Play using the account that bought them. Nikfort Games cannot remotely access or erase locally saved puzzles.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to send. We use it to respond and retain correspondence as needed for support or legal obligations. To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of correspondence we hold, email nikfortgames@gmail.com.

This website and policy updates

The game opens this policy in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts it and handles information needed to serve the page under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Nikfort Games adds no advertising or analytics scripts to this page. If the game’s privacy practices change, we will update this page and the last-updated date.
