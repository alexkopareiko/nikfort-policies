This policy explains how information is handled when you use Orchid Care: Grow & Bloom, an Android orchid-care journal.

Developer and Publisher:
OLEKSANDR KOPAREIKO operating under Nikfort Games brand

App: Orchid Care: Grow & Bloom
Android package: com.NikfortGames.orchid_care
Privacy contact: nikfortgames@gmail.com

Your journal is stored on your device. No app account is required. We do not operate a server that receives or synchronises your orchid collection. Optional purchases, external links, support messages, and files you choose to export involve the services described below.

1. Records and photos on your device

The app stores information you enter or select so that it can maintain your journal and display care guidance. This may include:

  • Orchid names, genera and groups, acquisition dates, pot and growing-medium details, and notes.
  • Growing places, light descriptions, temperature and humidity readings, and related notes.
  • Dated care observations, bloom stages, check schedules, and unfinished plant-editing drafts.
  • Photos you choose for your orchids, display preferences, and reminder settings.

Journal records are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated collection service. The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

When you choose a photo using Android's picker, the app creates a resized JPEG copy in private storage for display and backup. It does not copy the original file's EXIF metadata into the new JPEG. Your original photo remains in its original location. Photos and notes can contain personal information, so choose what you add and export accordingly.

2. Permissions and local reminders

The app uses Android's photo and document pickers for files you select. It does not request camera, contacts, location, or broad photo-library access.

Notifications are optional. When you enable reminders, the app may request Android notification permission. Local alarms provide a daily care-check reminder. The app can briefly receive alarm, device-restart, app-update and time-change broadcasts to maintain these reminders; it does not use a foreground service or background location tracking.

Notification content may reveal that you have orchids needing a check. You control notifications and lock-screen visibility through Android settings. Declining notifications does not remove journal records or the in-app list of due checks.

3. Optional Google Play purchases

The app offers an optional one-time Pro unlock through Google Play. It does not offer a subscription. Google processes checkout and payment details under its own terms and privacy policy.

The Google Play Billing integration communicates with Google Play and receives product, purchase and ownership information needed to complete, acknowledge and restore the unlock. The app stores purchase entitlement separately from your journal. We do not receive your payment-card details through the app.

Google may handle transaction, device and diagnostic information as part of providing its platform services. Local journal storage does not mean that Google Play makes no network requests or processes no data.

4. Backups, exports and selected destinations

You can create a free JSON backup containing your collection, growing places, care and bloom history, unfinished plant drafts, and their associated photos. The optional Pro features can produce PDF and CSV exports of selected records.

You choose where to save these files through Android's document picker. A selected destination may be local storage, an attached device or a cloud provider. That provider handles the saved file under its own terms and privacy practices. Opening an exported document in another app gives that app access to the document you open.

Backup and export files are not separately password-protected or encrypted by Orchid Care. They may contain private notes and images. Protect the files and any copies you share. The app's Android automatic-backup setting is disabled; device manufacturers and operating-system transfer features may have their own behavior.

Restoring a backup replaces the current journal after validation and retains a private pre-restore recovery copy. Purchase entitlement and notification opt-in are not granted by importing a backup. Temperature and reminder preferences are kept separately from the journal backup.

5. Retention and deletion

Journal information remains on your device until you change or delete it, replace the journal through restore, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android's storage behavior.

  • Deleting an orchid removes its journal record and associated care and bloom records from the active collection. Discarding a plant draft removes that draft from the active journal.
  • An unreferenced private photo copy can remain for at least one day. A subsequent successful record save cleans up unreferenced copies older than that grace period, which protects a newly selected photo awaiting draft persistence.
  • A recovery backup, or a preserved copy of an unreadable collection file, can retain earlier records and photos until replaced or explicitly removed.
  • Settings → Delete all local journal data removes the journal, drafts, private photos and recovery copies. Purchase ownership is kept separately.

Deleting data in the app does not remove original photos, previously exported files, copies saved with other providers, or Google's payment and transaction records. Remove external copies through the provider or location where you saved them.

There is no app-operated account or synchronised collection for us to delete remotely. We cannot access the journal kept privately on your device. For information you send to support, see section 8.

6. External services, links and this website

Care-source and privacy links open in your system browser. Visiting those pages sends the normal web request information to their operators, whose privacy practices apply. The app does not bundle the privacy-policy text or display a cached policy in an embedded browser.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and operate the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, external fonts, or contact forms.

Android, Google Play, a device manufacturer, or a file provider may process platform or diagnostic information independently of the journal. For example, Google Play may make app-quality reports available through Android vitals. Their handling is governed by their services and settings; this policy does not claim that all platform processing is disabled.

7. Security

The app uses Android's app-private storage for its own journal and photo copies. Device locking, system updates, and the security of destinations you choose for exported files help protect your information. The app does not add a separate journal password or its own encrypted backup format.

Only export or send records and images you intend to disclose. You do not need to send us your full collection to ask a general support question.

8. Support and privacy requests

Email nikfortgames@gmail.com and mention Orchid Care. If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond, investigate the issue, or handle your privacy request. The support mailbox is provided through Gmail, a Google service.

Support correspondence remains in our mailbox unless it is deleted. You may ask us to correct or delete information you sent to support; we may need to retain information required to resolve an ongoing issue or meet applicable legal obligations. Please identify the relevant conversation. Requests about on-device journal data must be carried out using the app or Android controls because we cannot remotely access those records.

No registration or age profile is requested in the app. A parent or guardian can use this contact address for questions about information sent to support.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data-handling practices change. The effective and last-updated dates appear at the top of this page. The current policy remains available at this address; material changes will be communicated as appropriate through this page, the app or its store listing.
