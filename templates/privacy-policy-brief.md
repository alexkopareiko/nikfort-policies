# New app policy brief — authoring only

Do not publish this checklist or a page with unfilled fields. Use the current app's actual source, release configuration, SDK disclosures and runtime evidence.

- App title, permanent package ID, publisher identity and working contact.
- Effective date and revision date; existing URLs/old versions that must remain reachable.
- Each record/photo/attachment type; source, purpose, storage and any transfer/recipient.
- Private copies, original files, EXIF or other metadata, drafts, crash/recovery copies, caches and snapshots.
- Account/sign-in, own servers, external services, advertising, analytics, diagnostics and purchase SDKs.
- Permissions and background behavior; notifications and lock-screen disclosure.
- Manual backup/export/share, user-selected cloud destinations, encryption/password protection, automatic Android backup/device transfer.
- Exact deletion controls; what remains in recovery, external copies, originals, transactions and support correspondence.
- Security measures actually present; no unverified absolute claims.
- Support handling/contact, applicable requests and age/children-related practices actually relevant to the app.
- Third-party service privacy links and hosting provider handling of website requests.
- Current Google Play requirements and candidate-specific Data safety assessment; no inherited blanket declaration.
- Full anonymous HTTPS page check, readable mobile layout, real installed-app link, and Console field separately recorded.

Use a permanent route `/<app-slug>/privacy/`. Publish terms or account-deletion instructions only where applicable and review their actual requirements independently.
